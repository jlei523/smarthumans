## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**LLM assessment:** This does not read as AI-generated. The identity is committed and specific: newsprint surfaces, hairline rules, the seven-verdict pill system, serif-for-content / sans-for-UI / mono-for-numerals discipline. The small-caps section header is a coherent system (it IS the heading, never an eyebrow above one). One honest caveat: the warm-paper background sits at the edge of the "AI cream default" band and the tokens are literally named `--paper-2/3` — it survives because the full newsprint system (typography, rules, verdict pills) earns it, not because the tint does.

**Deterministic scan (CLI, 2 findings):** (1) side-tab: 8px `borderLeft` in the OG share-card route (`src/app/api/og/claim/[slug]/route.tsx:97`) — a verdict-color bar on the social image, deliberate but still the banned shape; (2) em-dash overuse: 6 em-dashes in layout.tsx body copy.

## Overall Impression

This is a real product with a real voice — the verdict-pill system, ledger density, and quote-as-artifact treatment land exactly on the data-journalism brief, and the edge states (not-yet-scoreable, request-this-person, min-sample notes) show unusual care. The single biggest opportunity is finishing the flagship: the person scorecard is missing its two spec'd centerpiece modules (stacked distribution bar, timeline view), and the one token that carries all metadata text fails WCAG AA everywhere at once.

## What's Working

- **The verdict system.** Pale pill + darkened text + always-a-label is colorblind-safe, instantly scannable, and unique to this site. The hatched "Disputed" badge reads even in grayscale.
- **Typographic discipline.** Serif only for what was said, Inter for the machinery, tabular mono for every number — it produces genuine FiveThirtyEight-grade authority, and the claim-detail quote artifact is the best moment on the site.
- **Container-query claim cards.** Cards adapt to their own width (shedding date, chip, long labels), so mobile holds up with no parallel layouts. The mobile ledger is clean.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] All metadata text fails WCAG AA, site-wide.** `#8b8579` (`--ink-3` / `--muted-foreground`) measures 3.3:1–3.7:1 at 11–12px: dates, "resolved 2h ago", audit-trail meta, venue lines, section headers, placeholders, helper copy. ~45 detector hits across 4 pages; PRODUCT.md commits to AA.
   **Fix:** darken the token to ≈`#6c675c` (≥4.5:1 on all three paper surfaces) in globals.css; reserve `--ink-4` for decoration only. One token change fixes most of the site.
   **Suggested command:** /impeccable polish

2. **[P1] The flagship scorecard under-delivers its own spec.** No stacked status-distribution bar (record is dots + counts), no timeline view toggle on the ledger (both spec'd in CLAUDE.md §2B), and the accuracy sparkline label overflows its box by 17px.
   **Fix:** build the stacked bar into the scorecard panel (it's also the compare-page primitive), add the list/timeline toggle to the ledger sort bar, fix the SVG text overflow in charts.tsx.
   **Suggested command:** /impeccable shape person scorecard (then craft)

3. **[P2] Verdict colors leak into non-verdict UI.** Header bell: hardcoded `bg-st-false` red dot shown to everyone including signed-out (fake urgency — an explicit PRODUCT.md anti-reference); homepage "Call of the week" card: 3px green side-stripe (`border-l-st-true`, also the banned side-stripe shape); /submit callouts: `border-l-2 border-foreground` and `border-l-2 border-st-pending` (page.tsx:69,77).
   **Fix:** bell dot only with a real unread count, in ink not red; restyle the three stripe callouts with full hairline borders + paper-2 tint or a leading icon.
   **Suggested command:** /impeccable quieter

4. **[P2] Compare page can't actually compare.** The per-category rows differ in set and order between the two people (Trump: Economy/Foreign Policy/…, Musk: AI/Other/Stocks/…), so nothing lines up; Trump's record line truncates mid-word ("2 pendi"). 100%/0% cells carry no sample-size context.
   **Fix:** render the union of categories in one shared row order with "—" for no data; let the record line wrap; add n= to category cells.
   **Suggested command:** /impeccable layout compare

5. **[P2] Copy drift at the edges.** Resolved claims still say "12k following — get notified when this resolves" (claims/[slug]/page.tsx:277); signed-out stance taps save locally with zero feedback ("kept locally, scored when you sign in" is never said); claim rationale and submit helper prose run 89–112ch (cap is ~75ch); "100% of 2 users say Yes" reads as false precision at tiny n.
   **Fix:** conditional follow-rail copy, a one-line local-stance hint, max-width on prose blocks, count-based phrasing under n=10.
   **Suggested command:** /impeccable clarify

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (Power User):** No keyboard surface at all — no "/" to focus search, no j/k in ledgers, no skip link. The header RSS icon opens /feed (a member feed), breaking the icon's promise of an RSS feed. Ledger filters are single-select dropdown menus that close after each pick — refining status+type+topic is three round-trips.

**Sam (Screen reader / keyboard):** No skip-to-content link — every page costs ~19 tab stops (header + topic strip) before main content. Header feed/bell buttons are icon-only with `title` attributes instead of `aria-label`. The ~45 sub-AA contrast hits hit low-vision users hardest; status dots rely on `title` for meaning (though labels appear nearby in most contexts).

**Casey (Mobile, one-handed):** Stance Yes/No buttons are ~24px tall and the follow pill ~26px — both below the 44pt floor and adjacent to each other inside cards. Search requires a tap on the magnifier first (the core action is two steps). Mobile cards shed the date entirely, so a ledger sorted by "All time" gives no temporal cues.

## Minor Observations

- Homepage has no `h1` and its largest text is 19px — fine visually, but weak for semantics/SEO on the most-linked page.
- Compare subtitle "Same template for everyone — the data delivers the verdict." is the one slogan-flavored line on a product surface (the no-marketing-copy rule applies).
- The OG share card's 8px left bar: consider a top-edge verdict bar so the no-side-stripe rule stays absolute everywhere.
- Em-dash density in UI copy is high (6 in layout metadata alone) — an AI-cadence tell worth thinning in helper text.
- "EDITORIAL STANDARD" kicker on /methodology is the site's only eyebrow — acceptable as a one-off; don't let it spread.
- Leaderboard explainer line packs three formulas into one muted sentence — candidates for a footnote.
- Leaderboard topic pills show 15 options at once (working-memory ceiling is ~4; the row is scannable but consider collapsing the tail under "More").
